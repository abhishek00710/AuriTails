AuriTails

Privacy Policy

AuriTails is a local-first pet care companion for profiles, routines, wellness records, memories, reminders, and Care Circle sharing. This policy explains what information the app may handle and how it is used.

Effective date: May 22, 2026

Information You Provide

You may choose to enter owner details, pet details, routines, behavior check-ins, weight entries, medications, symptoms, vaccine records, medical history, food notes, memories, photos, and scanned documents. This information is used to power the app features you choose to use.

Local-First Storage

AuriTails is designed to keep core pet data and media on your device by default. If you delete the app, local-only data may be deleted unless you exported a backup or enabled a supported cloud feature.

Cloud Features

Optional cloud features, such as Care Circle sharing and account restore, may use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Firebase Storage. When you sign in or enable these features, selected app data may be associated with your Firebase account so it can be restored or shared with invited caregivers.

Photos, Camera, Location, and Notifications

AuriTails may request access to Photos, Camera, Location, and Notifications only when needed for features such as memory photos, document scanning, nearby pet care lookup, reminders, or location-based pet care convenience. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

AuriTails may use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app reliability and improve the product. We aim to avoid logging raw personal notes, medical free text, or sensitive pet records in analytics events.

Sharing

Your pet information is not public by default. Information is shared only when you explicitly share content, export a backup, or invite trusted caregivers into a Care Circle or similar cloud-backed feature.

Health and Veterinary Disclaimer

AuriTails helps organize pet care information and summaries. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or replace advice from a licensed veterinarian. For medical decisions or emergencies, contact a veterinarian or emergency clinic.

Third-Party Services

Some features rely on Apple system services and Google Firebase services. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies.
