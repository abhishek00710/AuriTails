# AuriTails Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how `AuriTails` collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

This document is a practical product policy draft for the current app implementation and roadmap. It should be reviewed and finalized before public launch.

## Summary

`AuriTails` is designed to be local-first. Most pet records, routines, memories, and uploaded media are stored on the device by default.

If cloud features such as `Care Circle`, Firebase authentication, analytics, crash reporting, or shared media are enabled, some information may also be processed by third-party service providers described below.

## Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly

You may provide:

- Owner profile details such as name, location, headline, and notes
- Pet profile details such as name, species, breed, age, weight, and preferences
- Wellness data such as vaccines, medications, symptoms, food notes, weight logs, and medical history
- Routine and schedule information
- Memories, captions, and optional photos
- Care Circle member details such as caregiver names, contact information, and invite metadata

### Media and documents

If you choose to upload or scan content, `AuriTails` may process:

- Owner, pet, and bond photos
- Memory photos
- Vaccine certificates
- Imported vet or wellness documents

### Device permissions

Depending on the features you use, `AuriTails` may request access to:

- `Photos`
  to select profile images, memory photos, and vaccine certificates
- `Camera`
  to scan vaccine certificates and vet documents
- `Location`
  to find nearby pet hospitals and veterinary care
- `Notifications`
  to send reminders for routines, vaccines, medications, and memory celebrations

### Automatically generated app data

The app may generate or infer:

- Bond Pulse and wellness insight summaries based on the information you enter
- Activity timestamps and change history for local records
- Local backup/export files when you choose to create them

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate the app’s pet care, wellness, memory, and routine features
- Generate on-device summaries, trend views, and Bond Pulse insights
- Help you organize pet health and lifestyle records
- Send local reminders when enabled
- Support document import, scanning, and PDF export features
- Enable optional shared features such as Care Circle when cloud services are configured
- Maintain app reliability, security, and debugging

## Local Storage

`AuriTails` is designed to store core pet and owner data locally on the device.

That local data may include:

- owner profile data
- pet profile data
- behavior, weight, routine, and wellness records
- memories and associated local media
- backup files you choose to export

If the app is deleted, locally stored app data may also be deleted from the device unless you created a separate backup or synced eligible data to a cloud service.

## Cloud Services and Third Parties

### Firebase

If cloud features are enabled, `AuriTails` may use Google Firebase services, including:

- `Firebase Authentication`
  for sign-in and account identity
- `Cloud Firestore`
  for shared Care Circle data
- `Firebase Storage`
  for eligible shared media
- `Firebase Analytics`
  for usage analytics
- `Firebase Crashlytics`
  for crash and error reporting

Firebase is operated by Google and is subject to Google’s privacy practices:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Terms and Privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

Cloud features should be treated as optional and may not be enabled in every build or release.

### Apple Services

`AuriTails` may also rely on Apple frameworks and services such as:

- MapKit and location services
- Photos and camera frameworks
- local notification services
- system share sheets, file import/export, and document scanning

## Sharing of Information

We do not treat personal or pet information as public content by default.

Information may be shared only:

- when you explicitly use a share feature
- when you invite a trusted caregiver into `Care Circle`
- when cloud syncing is enabled for a shared feature
- when required by law or legal process
- when necessary to protect users, the app, or the public

## Care Circle and Shared Data

If you use `Care Circle`, some pet-related information may become visible to invited caregivers in the shared space.

Examples may include:

- pet profile details
- routines
- wellness records
- medications and symptoms
- memories and selected shared media

For V1 planning, local storage remains primary, and cloud-shared media may be limited by Care Circle quota rules.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

If enabled, analytics and crash reporting may collect limited technical and usage information such as:

- app version
- device type
- feature usage events
- crash stack traces
- non-fatal error diagnostics

The app should avoid sending sensitive freeform notes or unnecessary health details into analytics events.

## Data Retention

We retain information:

- locally on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or overwrite it
- in backups until you delete those backup files
- in cloud services for as long as needed to provide enabled cloud features, subject to account deletion, record deletion, and provider retention rules

## Your Choices

You can choose to:

- edit or delete many in-app records
- archive or delete pets
- control reminder settings
- decline permission requests such as camera, location, or notifications
- export and restore backups where supported
- choose whether to use cloud sign-in and shared features when available

## Children

`AuriTails` is not intended for children to use independently. Pet records should be managed by a responsible adult or account holder.

## Security

We use reasonable efforts to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

You are responsible for:

- securing your device
- controlling who can access your account or Care Circle
- reviewing shared data before inviting others

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the `Last updated` date above.
